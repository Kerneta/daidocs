# DaiDocs Memory Capture: Acceptable Use

No em dashes, per project rule.

DaiDocs Memory Capture is provided for PERSONAL use: keeping your own private
memory of what you have read and discussed, on your own computer.

## Permitted

- Capturing content you browse for your own recall and personal productivity.
- Storing it locally, encrypting it, and reading it back yourself.

## Not permitted

- Selling, licensing, or redistributing captured data.
- Building profiles of, or datasets about, other people from their posts.
- Surveillance or monitoring of other people.
- Any commercial data-harvesting or scraping operation.
- Bypassing the sensitive-site exclusions or capturing without consent.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for how you use what the tool captures, and for complying
with the terms of the sites you visit and the laws that apply to you. The
personal/household nature of the intended use is what keeps it inside common
privacy-law exemptions; using it commercially or against other people removes
that protection and is not a supported use.
