DaiDocs Memory Capture: Privacy Policy

Everything this version captures stays in plain files on your own computer, and the developer never receives your captured data. This is the local version and it stays local; a separate cloud plan for collaboration may come later, opt-in and with its own add-on.

What is captured

Only when you turn capture on for a site and after you accept this policy: the displayed text of pages and posts you scroll past; for social posts the author, timestamp, text and permalink; for AI chats the conversation text.

What is never captured

What you type (form fields, drafts), passwords, sensitive sites (banking, payments, webmail, health, government, password managers, never-capture and cannot be overridden), and high-risk personal numbers (scrubbed). No audio, no screenshots.

Where it goes

Plain files in a folder you choose on your computer, optionally encrypted with a password you set. It is not transmitted off your machine; the capture server is localhost only. Conversion to searchable memory is local by default and never sends browsing content to a cloud model without your explicit choice.

Your control

Turn capture on/off per site or globally, add sites to your never-capture list, change the storage folder, encrypt and read back only with your password, and delete the files yourself anytime.

Personal use only

Captured posts may contain other people's data. This is for personal use only, not for collecting, profiling, selling or redistributing others' personal data.
