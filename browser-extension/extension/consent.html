DaiDocs Memory Capture

Please read this and accept before any capture begins.

What it does

  • Saves the content you scroll on sites you turn on, into plain files on your own computer.
  • This version runs on your computer, and the developer never receives your captured data.
  • You can encrypt it with a password (the vault) and choose where it is stored.

What it never does

  • It does not capture what you type, passwords, or form fields, only what is displayed.
  • It never captures banking, health, webmail, or password-manager sites.
  • It does not send your captured content anywhere off your machine.

Intended use

  • This is for your personal use only: your own memory of what you read.
  • It is not for selling data, harvesting other people's personal data, or surveillance.
  • You are responsible for how you use what it captures.
