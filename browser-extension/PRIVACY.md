# DaiDocs Memory Capture: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-23. Plain-English, because the whole point is that you can
understand what happens to your data. No em dashes anywhere, per project rule.

## The one-sentence version

Everything this version captures stays in plain files on your own computer, and
the developer never receives your captured data.

## Who processes your data

You do, on your own machine. The extension and its local companion (the capture
server) run entirely on your computer. The developer operates no server that
receives, stores, or processes your captured content. For the data the extension
captures, there is no remote data controller and no remote data processor.

## Local now, and this version stays local

This is the local version of DaiDocs, and it keeps your data on your computer. A
separate cloud plan for collaboration may be offered later. If it is, it would be
entirely opt-in, would use a different add-on that you install by choice, and
would not change how this local version behaves. If you never opt in, nothing you
capture here leaves your machine.

## What is captured

Only when you turn capture on for a site, and only after you accept this policy
on first install:

- The displayed text of pages and posts you actually scroll past.
- For social posts: author, timestamp, the post text, and the post's permalink.
- For AI chats (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini): the conversation text.

## What is never captured

- What you type: form fields, search boxes, message drafts, and anything in an
  editable area are excluded.
- Passwords and login fields.
- Sensitive sites: banking, payments, webmail, health, government, and password
  managers are on a built-in never-capture list and cannot be overridden.
- High-risk personal numbers (card numbers, national IDs) are scrubbed before
  anything is stored.
- No audio. No screenshots. No screen recording.

## Where your data goes

- To plain files in a folder on your computer that you choose (default a DaiDocs
  folder in your home directory).
- You can encrypt it with a password (the vault). The password is never stored.
- It is not transmitted off your machine by the extension or the capture server.
  The capture server listens on localhost only.

## The conversion step (important)

Captured files can later be converted into searchable memory. By default this is
done locally with no external service. Converting captured browsing content
through a third-party AI model is not done automatically and would only happen if
you explicitly choose it. Captured browsing content is not sent to any cloud
model by default.

## Your control

- Turn capture on or off per site, or globally.
- Add any site to your own never-capture list.
- Choose and change the storage folder.
- Encrypt with a password and read files back only with that password.
- Delete the files yourself at any time: they are just files on your disk.

## Other people's data

Captured posts may contain other people's personal data (names, handles, text).
This tool is provided for personal use only. See ACCEPTABLE-USE.md. Using it to
collect, profile, sell, or redistribute other people's personal data is not a
permitted use.

## Children

Not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be shown in the extension before they take
effect, and you will be asked to accept again.
